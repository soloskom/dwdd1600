Who are we?

At the forefront of budget vacations worldwide, the titular Budget Vacations company has been the leader in finding affordable flights, trips, and hotels around the world! Say goodbye to stressful hopping between dozens of websites to find the best deals and relax with our preset, cost-effective packages. View the other tabs at the top of this page for more details.

Our history goes back for decades, ever since our founder Johnny Smithson decided to create an affordable and simple way to plan full vacations by phone. With the evolution of the internet, our services have only grown and grown—so naturally, we are more efficient and useful than ever right now!

How To Use

Pop in your starting location, preferred destination, and travel dates, and check out our low-cost vacation packages. It's really easy to compare prices and ratings and read customer reviews to find the right trip for you. You can also build your own vacation by choosing hotels, flights, and car rental separately—you save even more when you bundle them together on Budget Vacations. Try low season dates and avoid weekends to get the most out of your vacation under $3500.
